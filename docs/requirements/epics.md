## Initial Epics

Here I share my proposed initial set:

| ID | Epic | Purpose | Broad scope |
| --- | --- | --- | ---|
| E01 | Accounts and personal context | Give users access to their own records and relevant personal context | Account access, preferences, and optional history. |
| E02 | Weight tracking and reflection | Help users record and review weight changes over time. | Weight entries, history, notes, and trends. |
| E03 | Menstrual-cycle context | Help users explore weight records alongside cycle information | Period records, contextual comparisons, and clearly identified estimates. |
| E04 | Food and calorie tracking | Bring intake records into the logbook. | Food entries, calorie totals, and reusable foods/meals. |
| E05 | Movement tracking | Record movement alongside other observations. | Activity entries and historical review. |
| E06 | AI-assisted exploration | Help users explore their records and access educational context. | Summaries, questions, reflection prompts, and explanations with uncertainty. |

## Note
> This is an initial capability map. The current transition focuses on account access and viewing existing weight history. Detailed stories for the remaining capabilities will be developed as they approach implementation.
